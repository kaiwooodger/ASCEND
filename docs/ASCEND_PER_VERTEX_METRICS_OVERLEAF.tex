\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Primary vertex dose and geometry QA}}\\*
\addlinespace[2pt]
Vertex identifier & Per vertex & --- & Stable label linking the validated vertex mask, QA record, graph node and exported evidence. \\
Applicability/status & Per vertex & category & States whether the vertex mask and required inputs support calculation; unavailable quantities remain explicitly unavailable rather than being replaced by zero. \\
Geometric volume & Per vertex & cm$^{3}$ & Physical volume occupied by the validated vertex mask on the native RTDOSE grid. \\
Geometric centroid & Per vertex & mm (LPS) & Three-dimensional centroid of the vertex mask in DICOM patient left--posterior--superior coordinates. \\
$D_{\mathrm{mean}}$ & Per vertex & Gy & Arithmetic mean absorbed dose across all native-dose voxels within the vertex mask. \\
$D_{95\%}$ & Per vertex & Gy & Near-minimum coverage dose; 95\% of the vertex volume receives this dose or more. \\
$D_{\max}$ & Per vertex & Gy & Largest stored absorbed-dose value within the vertex mask. \\
$V_{95\%,Rx_H}$ & Per vertex & \% & Percentage of the vertex volume receiving at least $0.95\,Rx_H$, where $Rx_H$ is the configured high-dose prescription. \\
$0.95\,Rx_H$ threshold & Per vertex & Gy & Absolute dose threshold used to calculate $V_{95\%,Rx_H}$; unavailable when a valid $Rx_H$ has not been configured. \\
Maximum-dose voxel & Per vertex & $(z,y,x)$ & Native RTDOSE array index of the vertex-local maximum-dose voxel used to position the FWHM sampling axes. \\
Local FWHM & Per vertex & mm & Arithmetic mean of the valid native X-, Y- and Z-axis full widths at half maximum through the vertex-local maximum-dose voxel. \\
Native X/Y/Z FWHM & Per vertex & mm & Axis-specific linearly interpolated half-maximum widths; retained separately so directional variation is not hidden by the mean. \\
Half-maximum dose & Per vertex & Gy & Threshold used for FWHM, defined as 50\% of the dose at the vertex-local maximum-dose voxel. \\
Nearest vertex & Per vertex & identifier & Vertex whose centroid has the smallest three-dimensional Euclidean distance from the current vertex centroid. \\
Nearest-neighbour distance & Per vertex & mm & Centroid-to-centroid distance to the nearest vertex; this is not a surface-to-surface separation. \\
All inter-vertex distances & Per vertex & mm & Stored centroid-to-centroid distances from the current vertex to every other resolved vertex. \\
QA warnings & Per vertex & category & Explicit limitations, including an invalid mask, missing prescription for $V_{95\%,Rx_H}$ or unavailable FWHM for a zero-dose vertex. \\

\addlinespace[4pt]
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Global FWHM summary}}\\*
\addlinespace[2pt]
Valid-vertex count & Plan & count & Number of vertices contributing a valid local FWHM value to the global summary. \\
Average FWHM & Plan & mm & Arithmetic mean of all valid local vertex FWHM values. \\
Median FWHM & Plan & mm & 50th percentile of all valid local vertex FWHM values. \\
Minimum FWHM & Plan & mm & Smallest valid local vertex FWHM observed in the plan. \\
Maximum FWHM & Plan & mm & Largest valid local vertex FWHM observed in the plan. \\
Summary status & Plan & category & Records whether at least one valid local FWHM value was available for aggregation. \\

\addlinespace[4pt]
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{ICRU Report 91-context dose-gradient outputs}}\\*
\addlinespace[2pt]
High-dose prescription $Rx_H$ & Plan & Gy & Configured high-dose prescription and its recorded source; ASCEND does not infer this value from the dose maximum. \\
Half-prescription isodose volume ($PIV_{\mathrm{half}}$) & Plan & cm$^{3}$ & Physical native-grid volume receiving at least $0.5\,Rx_H$. \\
Prescription isodose volume ($PIV$) & Plan & cm$^{3}$ & Physical native-grid volume receiving at least $Rx_H$. \\
Paddick Gradient Index ($GI$) & Plan & ratio & Whole-plan dose-falloff index, $GI=PIV_{\mathrm{half}}/PIV$. Lower values indicate steeper fall-off only when target volume and conformity are comparable. \\
Isodose-volume profile & Plan & cm$^{3}$ & Volumes receiving at least 25\%--125\% of $Rx_H$, sampled in 5\% increments; the 50\% and 100\% points define $GI$. \\
Profile dose threshold & Plan & Gy & Absolute dose corresponding to each relative $Rx_H$ level in the isodose-volume profile. \\
Profile voxel count & Plan & count & Number of native RTDOSE voxels meeting or exceeding each isodose threshold. \\
High-dose target volume & Plan & cm$^{3}$ & Aggregate physical volume of the resolved high-dose vertex masks, stored as interpretation context for $GI$. \\
Individual target volumes & Plan & cm$^{3}$ & Physical volumes of the resolved high-dose targets, including the minimum target volume used to flag sub-cm$^{3}$ interpretation context. \\
Number of targets & Plan & count & Number of resolved high-dose targets contributing to the whole-plan dose distribution and combined low-dose wash. \\
Gradient calculation status & Plan & category & Records whether $GI$ was calculated or was unavailable because $Rx_H$ was missing or no voxel reached the prescription dose. \\
Gradient QA warnings & Plan & category & Flags 50\% or 100\% isodose contact with the RTDOSE-grid boundary, multiple-target low-dose overlap and sub-cm$^{3}$ target context. \\

\multicolumn{4}{p{15.15cm}}{\footnotesize\textit{Notes:} FWHM denotes full width at half maximum; GI denotes Gradient Index; PIV denotes prescription isodose volume; and LPS denotes the DICOM left--posterior--superior patient coordinate system. ASCEND 1.6.1 does not calculate or report the retired per-vertex radial-shell profile, $r_{80}$, $r_{50}$, $r_{20}$, radial penumbra, radial Gy/mm gradient or profile-anisotropy outputs. Paddick GI is a whole-plan descriptor and is not apportioned to individual vertices. Layer 2.2 midpoint, saddle and iPVDR quantities remain available but are outside the vertex-and-gradient scope of this table.}\\
